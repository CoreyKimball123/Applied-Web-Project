Reports to: Nurse Unit Manager

(Nursing Students strongly recommended to apply)

Ref Number: AB4G5

Job Description

At Cool Kids Medical, we believe everyone should have access to good professionals to support their needs, no matter where they are. So this role is fully remote. You’ll guide clients in building healthier habits, provide general wellness support, and help them stay on track with their personal health goals.

Salary: $80,000 - $90,000

Reports to: Clinical Services Manager

Applicants with a background in fitness, mental health support, or community care are encouraged to apply

Essential and preferable requirements

  • Certificate III or IV in Health, Fitness, Community Services (Any related disciplines)
  • Have minimum 1 year experience working in Community services or Health coaching
  • Police check (National Police Certificate)
  • Ability to work independently in a remote environment

Key responsibilities

  1. You will help clients set their goals using your knowledge, and support them with planning their schedule so they can actually achieve them.
  2. You will give professional advice to help improve their physical, emotional, social, and overall wellbeing.
  3. You will make sure to document everything properly and keep accurate records of each client, and know when to escalate concerns to other medical professionals.
  4. You will have a set schedule of clients that you’ll check in on regularly to see how they’re going.
